# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router, React, and TypeScript. ASP.NET Core on .NET 11, PostgreSQL, Keycloak, Elsa, RabbitMQ, Redis, and operator-managed n8n.

## Users

Employees submit requests. Finance and operations teams route work, review spending, and audit decisions. Managers approve work assigned to them. Organization administrators manage membership and policy.

## Product Purpose

FlowOps gives organizations one workspace to define, run, and monitor internal processes. The first acceptance scenario is a purchase request that survives a service restart while waiting for the assigned approver.

## Operating Context

Use a tenant-scoped workspace. A requester submits details and an approver decides with the amount, context, and history in view. Operations teams trace the outcome to a versioned workflow and its audit trail.

## Product Principles

- Make the current owner and next action visible.
- Preserve the record of every material decision.
- Validate definitions before publishing and keep published versions immutable.
- Enforce membership and permissions on the server.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Elsa executes business workflows. n8n handles operator-managed integrations within its licensing terms. English is the default language and Turkish is supported. Pricing and launch evidence are open decisions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Support keyboard operation, reduced motion, accessible light and dark themes, and desktop through mobile layouts.
